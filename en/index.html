Technical manifesto

What is the STEM Racing Challenge

An international science, technology and engineering education challenge supported by Formula 1® and coordinated in Mexico by Persiste A.C., where we operate as a real racing team, start to finish.

We design a CO₂-powered car using professional engineering tools (CAD, CAM, CFD) under a strict technical regulation.

We run the team like a real company: managing budget, brand identity and business plans, aiming to represent Mexico at the world final.

Season goal: stand out at the regional stage and secure qualification for the STEM Racing Mexico 2027 National Final, with a path to the FIA World Final.

Judging

What gets judged

Car engineering, track speed, business plan, brand identity (pit display) and an oral presentation before judges.

Scope

Why it matters

The challenge integrates CAD modeling, aerodynamic validation in CFD and precision CNC manufacturing, all under an official technical regulation.

Mission

Real engineering, starting in high school.

Design, manufacture and race a scale F1 car, building technical and management skills at a professional level.

Vision

A team that sustains itself season after season.

Establish Striker Racing as a benchmark team for Preparatoria Celta within STEM Racing Mexico, with a technical and management foundation passed on to the next generations of the team, opening the path to the FIA World Final.

Tech stack

What we use to design the car

The software we need is free for students; what sponsors fund is the physical kit, equipment and logistics, not licenses.

Autodesk Fusion Free

Official CAD/CAM/CAE partner of STEM Racing. This is where we model the car part by part and prepare machining toolpaths.

Ansys Student Free

Includes Ansys Discovery, the tournament's official CFD partner. We simulate airflow over the car before manufacturing each version.

Computing equipment

CFD simulations demand equipment with strong graphics capability: this is one area where a sponsor's support directly speeds up our work.

Official STEM Racing Mexico kit

Chassis material blocks, CO₂ tanks, wheels, axles and eyelets, provided when the season registration fee is paid.

Frequently asked questions

Can sponsorship be in kind instead of cash?

Yes. Manufacturing materials, computing equipment, design, printing or transport count as a contribution and are recognized at the equivalent tier.

How will I know what my contribution was used for?

We send a short report after each stage (regional and national) with the car's progress, results and photos of the pit display featuring your brand.

Can I sponsor just part of the process, not the whole season?

Yes, tiers are flexible. We can adjust the benefits to a one-off contribution, for example just covering travel expenses for the national final.

Is the sponsorship tax deductible?

We run a commercial and advertising sponsorship scheme with a deductible invoice issued under the applicable regime for partner companies, coordinated through the corresponding institutional backing.
