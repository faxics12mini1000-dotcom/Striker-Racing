Frequently asked questions

Can sponsorship be in kind instead of cash?

Yes. Manufacturing materials, computing equipment, design, printing or transport count as a contribution and are recognized at the equivalent tier.

How will I know what my contribution was used for?

We send a short report after each stage (regional and national) with the car's progress, results and photos of the pit display featuring your brand.

Can I sponsor just part of the process, not the whole season?

Yes, tiers are flexible. We can adjust the benefits to a one-off contribution, for example just covering travel expenses for the national final.

Is the sponsorship tax deductible?

We run a commercial and advertising sponsorship scheme with a deductible invoice issued under the applicable regime for partner companies, coordinated through the corresponding institutional backing.
