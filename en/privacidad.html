Privacy notice

Last updated: September 2026

Striker Racing is the student team from Preparatoria Celta competing in the STEM Racing México challenge. This notice explains what happens with the information you share when contacting us through this site.

What data we receive

This site has no forms or database of its own. We only receive personal data when you decide to write to us directly through:

  • WhatsApp (name, phone number, and the message you send)
  • Email (name, email address, and the message content)

That information goes directly to the team's WhatsApp or Gmail, handled under Meta's and Google's respective privacy policies. Striker Racing does not sell, rent, or share that data with third parties.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics tools. Fonts are served from the site itself, with no third-party calls.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to delete a conversation or email you sent us by writing to strikerracingf1@gmail.com.

This notice covers what the website does today. Data handling for sponsors under formal agreements (billing, contracts) follows what is agreed directly with the institutional backing of Preparatoria Celta.
